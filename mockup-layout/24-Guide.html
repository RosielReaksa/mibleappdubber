Hide old subtitles with Blur
Guide 4 of 7 · Editing
1
Open your video in the editor
Tap Edit video on your finished project.
2
Open the Blur tool
Swipe the tool bar at the bottom and tap Blur.
3
Choose where
Tap Top, Bottom, Left, or Right. You can choose more than one.
4
Adjust and export
Make the strip a little bigger than the old text, then tap Export.
